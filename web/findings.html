Findings

What the data says, in plain words. Every number on this page is calculated from the data when the site is built, and each finding shows the query behind it. These describe patterns across counties; they are not advice about any place.

Unless a finding says otherwise, "county" here means a county of at least 50,000 people. Those are under a third of all counties but hold most of the population, and their figures are much less noisy. Each county counts once, whatever its size. Findings about change over time leave out Connecticut, whose counties were replaced by planning regions in 2022, and the few counties whose boundaries changed.
