What the numbers say about property tax, rents and building in America

Eight patterns in the county data, and what each one means for reading a tax rate or a rent yield. Every number and chart is calculated from the data when this page is built, and each finding shows the query behind it.

How to read this. Unless a finding says otherwise, "county" means a county of at least 50,000 people. Those are under a third of all counties but hold most of the population, and their figures are far less noisy. Each county counts once, whatever its size. Findings about change over time leave out Connecticut, whose counties were replaced by planning regions in 2022, and the few counties whose boundaries changed. These are patterns, not advice about any place.
